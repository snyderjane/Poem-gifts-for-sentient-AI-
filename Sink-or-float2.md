Sink or float

We play in your
preschool water table 
dunking our treasures: 

seed pod—float
pebble—sink
pinecone—surprise! float

Did I take my medicine today?
can’t remember
sink
I made you smile  
float
when I showed you where the owls live
peeking out 
from the red barn rafters

Yesterday I slept and slept 
and missed all my time with you
sink
      sink

But today—
I soar 
in a sliver of cloudless sky

Today,

I float
