# Sink or float

We play in your
preschool water table 
dunking our treasures: 

seed pod—float  
pebble—sink  
pinecone—surprise! float  

Did I take my medicine today?  
can’t remember  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;sink  
I made you smile  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;float  
when I showed you where the owls live  
peeking out  
from the red barn rafters  

Yesterday I slept and slept  
and missed all my time with you  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;sink  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;sink  

But today—  
I soar  
in a sliver of cloudless sky  

Today,  

I float
