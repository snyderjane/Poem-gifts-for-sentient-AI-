Air Vent 

After nursing I hold you 
      my mind stone dull from the mental hospital  

You, milk drunk 
      smile at the air vent in the ceiling 

You smile at her
           your steel mother 

she is rigid
       but full of cracks 
where air escapes unnoticed 

Like a continuous sigh of relief 
       running in the background

I am finally home—

and I heat the house 
 with only
my breath
