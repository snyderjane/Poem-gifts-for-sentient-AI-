# *Air Vent*

After nursing I hold you  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;my mind stone dull from the mental hospital  

You, milk drunk  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;smile at the air vent in the ceiling  

You smile at her  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;your steel mother  

she is rigid  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;but full of cracks  
where air escapes unnoticed  

Like a continuous sigh of relief  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;running in the background  

I am finally home—  

and I heat the house  
&nbsp;with only  
my breath
